## Bulk Description Templates

### Template A — Packaging Directory (300 words)
SupplePac is a premium sustainable packaging studio and manufacturing partner headquartered in Monroe, New York. Founded in 2020, we specialize in custom packaging for supplement, mushroom, hydration, biohacking, and premium DTC wellness brands.

Our packaging formats include stand-up pouches (matte, gloss, kraft, specialty film), HDPE and PET bottles, amber and clear glass jars, folding cartons, rigid boxes, shrink sleeves, pressure-sensitive labels, and branded shipping mailers.

We offer sustainable material options including post-consumer recycled (PCR) plastics, certified compostable films, FSC-certified paperboard, mono-material flexible structures, soy-based inks, and biodegradable mailers. Material-level certifications (FSC, BPI compostability, recycled content verification) are available on request.

Minimum order quantities start at 1,000 units for flexible formats and 500 units for rigid boxes and folding cartons. Standard lead time is 4–8 weeks from artwork approval, with rush programs available for select formats.

SupplePac provides supplement label design including FDA-compliant supplement facts panels, international regulatory guidance (UK trading standards, Australian TGA, Health Canada NHP bilingual labels), curated sample kits, and dieline templates.

We ship globally to the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, and beyond. Request a quote at supplepac.com/quote or sample kits at supplepac.com/samples.
