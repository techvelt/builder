**Example response:**

> *What should new supplement brands know about packaging minimum order quantities?*

Most supplement packaging converters set MOQs at 5,000–25,000 units, which forces early-stage brands to overcommit inventory on an unproven SKU. Low-MOQ programs starting at 1,000 units for pouches or 500 for cartons let founders treat their first production run as a market test rather than a bet. The per-unit premium at lower quantities is typically 15–30% — often less than the cost of sitting on 4,000 unsold units.

The tradeoff is lead time and tooling: at 1,000 units, expect 4–8 weeks from artwork approval and possible die-cutting fees ($120–260) that are often credited on repeat orders.
